<h1>Collect to Collect</h1>
<hr>
A small game I made for the Countdown theme where you click on falling balls to keep your timer alive and  survive..  some of them are safe, some straight up kill your time, and some are sneaky and flip on you mid-fall just to mess with you.
<hr>
<h3>Features:</h3>
<ol>
    <li>The whole game is based on the timer theme </li>
    <li>Click balls to gain time, miss them or click the wrong ones and you lose time</li>
    <li>Some balls trick you by changing color while falling so you can't just trust what you see at first</li>
    <li>Gets faster and more harder as  longer you survive</li>
</ol>
<hr>
<h3>How to Play:</h3>
<p>It's pretty simple, just:</p>
<ol>
    <li>Open the game and hit play</li>
    <li>Click the normal balls as they fall to gain time and score</li>
    <li>Avoid clicking the black ones, they take time away</li>
    <li>Watch out, safe looking balls can flip to black halfway down</li>
    <li>Don't just sit around either, ignoring a good ball costs you time too</li>
    <li>Survive as long as you can before the clock hits zero</li>
</ol>
